About The SACCO

A member-owned financial partner for disciplined saving and welfare support.

PPSW SACCO exists to strengthen the financial wellbeing of its members. The SACCO encourages regular saving, provides access to responsible credit, and keeps member records organized for quick service and clear accountability.
